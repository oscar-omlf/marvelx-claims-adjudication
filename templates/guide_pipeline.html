Claim processing flow

  1. Ingest claim inputs from a claim path, a full ZIP package, or a set of uploaded support files.
  2. Read source documents directly for text/markdown and via OCR for image-based evidence.
  3. Normalize multilingual text using accent stripping and lightweight concept mapping.
  4. Classify document roles into booking, medical, police, legal, description, or unknown.
  5. Extract structured facts from booking and itinerary-style files while preserving line structure.
  6. Score document signals across support, contradiction, hard invalidity, and soft uncertainty.
  7. Route the final decision according to explicit precedence in the policy engine.
  8. Persist and present the result through the stable API response shape and the internal inspection pages.

Why the extra benchmark files matter

Claims often require both the incident evidence and the itinerary or booking markdown file. The image alone usually proves the incident; the markdown file often proves that a covered trip, date, or booking actually existed.
