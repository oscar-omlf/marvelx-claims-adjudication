Decision policy & verification rules

The routing layer treats claim adjudication as a document-grounded decision problem, not a free-form text classification task.

Signal groups used by the router

  • Support: evidence that positively supports a covered reason.
  • Contradiction: authoritative evidence that defeats the claimed covered reason.
  • Hard invalidity: missing or unusable proof that should normally force denial.
  • Soft uncertainty: plausible but unresolved evidence that should avoid automatic approval.

Decision precedence

  1. Policy exclusion or uncovered reason
  2. Authoritative contradiction
  3. Hard invalidity or missing required proof
  4. Clear support without blockers
  5. Plausible support with unresolved uncertainty
